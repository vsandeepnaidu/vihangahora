Panchapakshi · பஞ்சபட்சி

Five birds. Five activities. One rhythm for your day.

Panchapakshi is a Tamil tradition that reads the texture of time through five birds. This site teaches it from the first idea to a full day’s reading, with every table and a live view of the present moment.

The cycle

Each bird moves through five activities

They run from strongest to weakest, and no two birds are ever in the same one.

Ruling

அரசு · strength 1

Full strength. The time for what matters most.

Eating

ஊண் · strength 0.8

Gaining and steady. Good for real work.

Walking

நடை · strength 0.6

On the move. Ordinary time.

Sleeping

துயில் · strength 0.4

At rest. Slow down and prepare.

Dying

சாவு · strength 0.2

Spent. Wait; it passes soon.

The day

A day is ten slots, from sunrise to sunrise

Five slots by day and five by night. In each your bird has a main activity, and each slot divides again into five shorter turns.

Know your bird

From your birth star and the half of the lunar month you were born in.

Read the slot

The half of the month and the weekday decide what your bird is doing in each slot.

Read the turn

Inside the slot, a turn of 12 to 48 minutes gives the activity of the moment.

Weigh the day

Ruling days lift everything; death days mute it. Friends and enemies adjust the rest.
