Lesson 12 of 14

A whole day, step by step

Ten steps applied to one bird on one day.

This lesson reads one complete day from start to finish, using only what the earlier lessons taught. The person is a Peacock. The day is a Friday in the bright half, with the 6-to-6 reckoning to keep the times round.

The ten steps

  1. Bird. Peacock (lesson 4).
  2. Half. The Moon is growing: bright half (lesson 5).
  3. Weekday. Friday, from sunrise on Friday to sunrise on the next day.
  4. Sunrise and sunset. 6:00 AM and 6:00 PM in this example. With real times you would look them up for your place.
  5. Slots. Five by day and five by night, each 2 h 24 min here.
  6. Main activities. From the Peacock’s table for a bright-half Friday (lesson 6). By day: Walking, Ruling, Sleeping, Dying, Eating. By night: Ruling, Dying, Walking, Sleeping, Eating.
  7. Kind of day. The Cock rules the day and the Vulture rules the night. It is the death day of the Owl. For the Peacock it is an ordinary day (lesson 8).
  8. Turns. Each slot divides into five turns, starting with its own activity (lesson 7).
  9. Sharing bird. For each turn, find the bird whose main activity matches, and whether it is a friend or an enemy (lessons 7 and 9).
  10. Grade. Apply the four rules (lesson 10).

Reading the result

Fifty turns is a lot of detail. In practice you scan for the good ones that fall in your waking hours. On this day, between 7 AM and 10 PM, they are:

  • 7:54 AM – 8:24 AM · Eating turn in a Walking slot, shared with the Cock (friend)
  • 8:24 AM – 9:12 AM · Ruling turn in a Ruling slot
  • 3:36 PM – 4:06 PM · Eating turn in a Eating slot
  • 6:00 PM – 6:24 PM · Ruling turn in a Ruling slot
  • 7:54 PM – 8:24 PM · Eating turn in a Ruling slot, shared with the Vulture (friend)

If the Peacock had one thing that mattered on this Friday and a free choice of when to do it, these are the stretches the tradition would point to. The rest of the day is for everything else.

Check your understanding

  • Pick any column of the day table’s slots and confirm the turns add up to 144 minutes.
  • Find a turn graded medium only because an enemy shares it.
  • Open the Today page, choose the Peacock, set the half to bright and pick a Friday: you should see this same day.

In short. Bird, half, weekday, sunrise, slots, main activities, kind of day, turns, sharing bird, grade. Ten steps, always in that order. After a few days the first seven take seconds.
