Lesson 6 of 14

Slots: the main activity

Two small rules that fill in every table.

In every slot each bird has a main activity. Two small rules produce all of them.

Rule 1 · The activities follow a fixed order

From one slot to the next, a bird moves one step along a set order of the five activities, and after the fifth it wraps round to the first. There are four such orders, one for each combination of half and day or night:

Bright half, day:
Eating → Walking → Ruling → Sleeping → Dying

Bright half, night:
Eating → Ruling → Dying → Walking → Sleeping

Dark half, day:
Eating → Dying → Sleeping → Ruling → Walking

Dark half, night:
Eating → Sleeping → Walking → Dying → Ruling

So if a bird is Eating in the first slot of a bright-half day, it is Walking in the second, Ruling in the third, Sleeping in the fourth and Dying in the fifth.

Rule 2 · The weekday sets the starting point

What each bird is doing in the first slot depends on the weekday. From there Rule 1 fills in the rest. Here is a whole bright-half Friday, by day, for all five birds:

Read along a row and you see one bird stepping through the order. Read down a column and you see the five birds holding five different activities, as lesson 3 promised.

Try any day

Change the half, day or night, and the weekday, and watch the pattern shift.

Some weekdays share a pattern. In the bright half, Sunday and Tuesday are alike, and so are Monday and Wednesday. In the dark half the pairs are Sunday with Tuesday and Monday with Saturday.

Every bird has four such tables: bright half by day and by night, dark half by day and by night. They are all on the bird pages.

The ruler. The bird that is Eating in the first slot is said to rule that whole day (or night). In the table above it is marked “rules”. Lesson 8 explains what that is worth.

In short. The weekday fixes each bird’s first slot. After that every bird steps through one of four fixed orders. That is the entire main-activity table.
