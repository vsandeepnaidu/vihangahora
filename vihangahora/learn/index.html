The course

Learn Pañcapakṣī

Fourteen lessons that build on each other. Start at the beginning; each one ends with a two-line summary.

  1. What Pañcapakṣī isFive birds, five activities, and the idea that time has texture.
  2. The five birdsVulture, Owl, Crow, Cock and Peacock, and what tradition links to each.
  3. The five activitiesRuling, Eating, Walking, Sleeping and Dying: a ladder of strength.
  4. Finding your birdFrom your birth star, or from the first sound of a name.
  5. How a day is laid outThe half of the month, the weekday from sunrise, and ten slots.
  6. Slots: the main activityTwo small rules that fill in every table.
  7. Turns: the finer rhythmFive unequal parts inside each slot, and the bird that shares them.
  8. Ruling days and death daysThe days that lift a bird, and the days that mute it.
  9. Friends and enemiesWhich birds help each other, and where that matters.
  10. Reading a momentPutting slot, turn, sharer and day together into one grade.
  11. Two people, one momentComparing two birds: the day, then strength.
  12. A whole day, step by stepTen steps applied to one bird on one day.
  13. The wider traditionWhat the old books add: beginnings, journeys, houses, directions and more.
  14. Using it wellDaily and weekly rhythm, common slips, and a month of practice.
