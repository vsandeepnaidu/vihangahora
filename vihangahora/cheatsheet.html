The method on one page

Everything you need to read a moment, in the order you use it. Made to be printed.

1 · Your bird

StarsFirst star of the groupBorn in the bright halfBorn in the dark half
1–5Ashwini …VulturePeacock
6–11Ardra …OwlCock
12–16Uttara Phalguni …CrowCrow
17–21Anuradha …CockOwl

No birth details? Use the first vowel sound of the name: a, i, u, e, o give Vulture, Owl, Crow, Cock, Peacock in the bright half. The dark half has its own list, and a name bird changes with the half.

2 · The five activities, strongest first

Ruling 1 › Eating 0.8 › Walking 0.6 › Sleeping 0.4 › Dying 0.2

3 · Fix the moment

  • Half: bright while the Moon grows, dark while it shrinks.
  • Weekday: from sunrise to the next sunrise. Before sunrise it is still the previous day’s night.
  • Slot: day and night are each cut into five equal slots (144 minutes at 6-to-6).
  • Turn: each slot holds five turns. The first repeats the slot’s activity; the rest follow the order below.

4 · The four orders and the turn lengths

Bright half, day
Eating 30 min → Walking 36 min → Ruling 48 min → Sleeping 18 min → Dying 12 min

Bright half, night
Eating 30 min → Ruling 24 min → Dying 36 min → Walking 30 min → Sleeping 24 min

Dark half, day
Eating 48 min → Dying 30 min → Sleeping 12 min → Ruling 18 min → Walking 36 min

Dark half, night
Eating 42 min → Sleeping 18 min → Walking 42 min → Dying 24 min → Ruling 18 min

From slot to slot a bird moves one step along the order. The weekday sets where it starts: see the bird pages.

7 · Grade the turn

  1. Sleeping or Dying turn: Bad
  2. Walking turn: Medium
  3. Ruling or Eating turn in a Sleeping or Dying slot: Medium
  4. Ruling or Eating turn in any other slot: Good, or Medium if an enemy shares it

The sharing bird of a turn is the bird whose main activity in that slot equals your turn’s activity.
