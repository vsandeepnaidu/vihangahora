Reference

Variants and sources

Where the traditional sources differ, and which reading this site follows.

Panchapakshi reached print through several routes: verse attributed to Agastya, a detailed English treatment based on a text ascribed to Pulippani, Tamil almanac booklets, and teachers’ notes. They agree on the structure and on nearly every number. In a handful of places they differ, mostly because of printing slips in tables that later books copied or corrected in different ways.

A site that teaches the method has to choose, and should say what it chose. This page does that.

Dark-half death daysVulture Tuesday; Owl Monday; Crow Sunday; Cock Thursday and Saturday; Peacock Wednesday and Friday.One Tamil booklet gives a different list (Vulture Monday; Owl Sunday; Crow Wednesday and Friday; Cock Tuesday; Peacock Thursday and Saturday).

Some books repair it by making the Crow the second enemy; reading it so that every friendship is mutual would make the Crow a friend and the Owl an enemy.
Bright-half day: Eating and Walking turnsEating 30 minutes, Walking 36.One text swaps them: Eating 36, Walking 30. The other three lengths agree everywhere.

Natural strength of the birdsCrow, Vulture, Owl, Cock, Peacock in the bright half; the Owl and Cock swap in the dark half.One set of notes puts the Owl above the Vulture.
Ruler and death day on the same nightTreated as neutral, in all three cases where it happens.The classical text states it for one case only.
Which half a day belongs toThis site: the half at the start of the day. The app: it switches at the exact moment the half changes.Common practice takes the lunar day at sunrise. One classical rule counts whole calendar days from the day after New or Full Moon.

What all the sources agree on

  • The five birds, their order and their vowels.
  • The five activities and their ranking.
  • The four orders of activities, and all turn lengths but one pair.
  • The first-slot activity of every bird on every weekday, in both halves.
  • The bright-half ruling days and death days, and the dark-half friends and enemies.
  • That a death day outranks a Ruling slot.

What is left out, on purpose

The old books contain more than timing. They also describe answering questions from the moment they are asked, readings connected with illness and other life events, lists of unfavourable months, remedies, diagrams and rites, some of them aimed against other people. This site leaves all of that out. It is not needed to understand or use the daily method, parts of it are harmful, and the texts themselves warn against misuse.

If you find a mistake

The tables here are generated from one data file, the same one the Vihanga Hora app uses. If your own source disagrees with something on this site, the difference is probably one of the points above. If it is not, we would like to know.
