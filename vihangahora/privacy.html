Privacy

Vihanga Hora has no accounts and no advertising. What you enter stays on your device.

The short version

  • You do not sign in, and we do not know who you are.
  • Your birth details, your bird, the people you add, your plans and your places are stored on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics are sent only if you switch them on. Both are off by default.
  • If you buy Vihanga Hora Plus, the purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play.
  • There is no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sale of data.

What the app keeps on your device

  • Your bird and how it was found: a birth star and half of the lunar month, the first sound of a name, or a bird you picked. Your birth date, time and place are kept only if you leave “remember my birth details” on.
  • Places you use for sunrise and sunset: a label, a time zone, and coordinates rounded to two decimal places (about a kilometre).
  • People you add for comparison: the label you type and their bird or star. If you enter someone’s birth details to find their bird, the details are used once and then discarded.
  • Plans, reminders and focus sessions you create, and your settings.

All of this is in the app’s own storage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

What can leave your device

WhatWhenWhat is sentWho receives it
Crash reportsOnly if you turn on “Share crash reports”The technical trace of the error, the app version, the operating system and the language. No birth details, names or birds.Sentry, our error-reporting provider
Usage statisticsOnly if you turn on “Share anonymous analytics”A short list of events, such as “a reminder was added”, with a random identifier that is not tied to you and is reset when you delete your data.PostHog, our analytics provider

Nothing else is sent. In particular, the app never sends your birth date, time or place, your name, the people you add, or anyone’s bird.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to work out sunrise and sunset. The calculation happens on your device. The rounded coordinates are stored there and are not sent to us. You can type a city instead.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. They do not pass through our servers, because there are none.

Your choices

  • Switches. “Share crash reports” and “Share anonymous analytics” can be turned on or off at any time in Me › Privacy.
  • Export. Me › Privacy › Export my data gives you a file with everything the app holds.
  • Delete. Me › Privacy › Delete all data erases the app’s data, cancels its reminders and resets the analytics identifier. A store purchase is not affected and can be restored.

Children

Vihanga Hora is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

This website

  • The site sets no cookies and has no analytics.
  • The bird you pick, your sunrise setting, your theme and your lesson progress are saved in your own browser so that the pages remember them. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.
  • If you choose “My location” on the Today page, your browser gives the page your position, which is used on your device to work out sunrise and is stored there rounded. It is not sent anywhere.
  • The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, those services receive your IP address when your browser fetches the files.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If the app starts to collect anything it does not collect today, it will ask first.
