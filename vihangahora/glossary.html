Glossary

The words used on this site, with their Tamil names.

TermTamilMeaning
Pañcapakṣīபஞ்சபட்சி“Five birds.” The timing system this site teaches.
Birdபட்சிOne of Vulture, Owl, Crow, Cock, Peacock. Each person has one.
Activityதொழில்What a bird is doing: Ruling, Eating, Walking, Sleeping or Dying.
RulingஅரசுThe strongest activity.
Eatingஊண்The second strongest activity.
WalkingநடைThe middle activity.
Sleepingதுயில்The second weakest activity.
DyingசாவுThe weakest activity.
Bright halfவளர்பிறைThe fortnight in which the Moon grows, from after New Moon to Full Moon. Sanskrit: śukla pakṣa.
Dark halfதேய்பிறைThe fortnight in which the Moon shrinks, from after Full Moon to New Moon. Sanskrit: kṛṣṇa pakṣa.
Slotஜாமம்One fifth of the day or of the night (yāmam). About 2 h 24 min.
Main activityA bird’s activity for a whole slot.
TurnOne of the five unequal parts of a slot (the sub-period). 12 to 48 minutes.
Sharing birdThe bird whose main activity equals your turn’s activity; it shares that turn with you.
Ruling dayA day (or night) ruled by the bird that is Eating in its first slot.
Death dayThe weekday on which a bird has no force, from sunrise to the next sunrise.
Friend, enemyநட்பு, பகைThe fixed relations between birds; they differ in the two halves.
Starநட்சத்திரம்One of 27 equal divisions of the Moon’s path (nakshatra). The birth star gives the birth bird.
Lunar dayதிதிOne thirtieth of the lunar month (tithi). Days 1 to 15 are the bright half, 16 to 30 the dark half.
Almanacபஞ்சாங்கம்A calendar giving the lunar day, star, sunrise and sunset for each date (pañcāṅgam).
6-to-6 reckoningTreating sunrise as 6:00 AM and sunset as 6:00 PM, so every slot is 144 minutes.
Natural strengthA fixed weight for each bird, used in the classical comparison of two people.
GradeGood, medium or bad: the summary of slot, turn, sharing bird and day.
